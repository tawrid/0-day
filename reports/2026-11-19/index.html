0-Day Vulnerabilities for 2026-11-19

CVE-2026-123890

VMware vCenter contains an authentication bypass vulnerability in the VMware Directory Service. Affected versions: 9.1.x (fixed in 9.1.1.0200); 9.0.x (fixed in 9.0.2.0200); 8.0 (fixed in 8.0 U3m); 5.x (fixed in 5.2.5). Telco Cloud Platform 3.0, 4.x, 5.0.x, 5.1.x, 5.2 and Telco Cloud Infrastructure 3.0 are also affected (see KB463116).
